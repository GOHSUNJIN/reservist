Help & FAQ
FOR RESERVISTS
How do I check in?
Open the Check-In tab and tap "Locate me" to verify your GPS. Once verified, tap Phase 1 to record your arrival. Complete Phase 2-4 as the day progresses.
What do the four phases mean?
Phase 1: Arrive and check in to work.
Phase 2: Leave for lunch or dinner break.
Phase 3: Return from break.
Phase 4: Check out at end of shift.
How do I request an absence?
Tap "Request absence" on the Check-In tab. Your supervisor will approve or decline, and you will be notified.
Can I check in if I'm offline?
Yes. Your check-in is queued and will sync automatically once your connection is restored.
What if the GPS fails?
Step outside to an open area with a clear view of the sky and try again. After several failed attempts a bypass option will appear. Use it only if GPS keeps failing, as bypassed check-ins are flagged for your supervisor.
What is the work timer and meal allowance?
When meal allowance is active for the cycle, a live work timer appears on the Check-In tab. Once you have worked 6 hours and recorded Phase 4, a "Meal eligible" badge appears. You must clock out (Phase 4) for the day to count toward your meal claim.
Why is there an orange warning on my calendar?
An orange day means you checked in but did not record a Phase 4 clock-out. Inform your supervisor so they can correct the record. Without a clock-out, the day will not count toward your meal allowance.
FOR ADMINS
How do I approve a new signup?
Go to the People tab and open the Requests sub-tab. Approve individually, or select multiple and tap "Approve selected".
How do I add someone manually?
Go to People tab, tap Roster, then tap the + in the Personnel section. For multiple people, use the Bulk button instead.
How do I export attendance?
Go to the Log tab. Tap Print for a printable PDF summary. Tap Excel for a spreadsheet download with per-person totals, attendance rates, and a Meal column showing how many days each person is eligible for meal allowance.
How do I post a notice to reservists?
Go to the Dashboard tab and tap Post notice to reservists. The message appears as a banner on every reservist's check-in screen for the duration of the cycle.
How do I reset a reservist's password?
Go to People tab, Roster sub-tab, find the person and tap Reset PW. Enter a temporary password and send it securely. They can update it in their account settings.
How do I find reservists who forgot to clock out?
Any reservist marked Present with no Phase 4 is flagged with a "No clock-out" badge on the Log tab. Correct their time using the manual time fields on their log card.
How do I see who the other supervisors are?
Go to the People tab and tap the Team sub-tab. It lists all active supervisors in your department with their contacts and roles.
FOR MASTER ACCOUNT
How do I switch between departments?
Use the DEPT dropdown in the header. Switching departments reloads all data for the selected department. Each department's last-selected cycle is remembered independently so switching back restores your previous context.
How do I add or remove a supervisor?
Go to the People tab and open the Team sub-tab. Tap Add supervisor to create a new admin account. To remove one, tap Remove on their card. They return to the reservist pool. You can also promote any existing reservist to supervisor from the same tab.
